What the business logic says now:
- Code computes the numbers. Each session is compared with the average of your last 3 sessions of the same type: pace, split drift, stroke-rate range, and DPS on water. Same input always gives the same numbers.
- A coaching judgment chooses the next session. It works from those numbers, your history, your goal and your comment, so the suggestion can differ between runs.
- Three fixed safety limits apply to every suggestion:
  - volume no more than 10% above the 3-session average;
  - a 32 spm cap after a clear DPS drop;
  - an easy session after 2 hard ones in a row (hard means 2:00/500 m or faster for your goal).
- If a suggestion breaks a limit, the app retries once. If it still fails, it shows a 30–40 min steady technique row at max 22 spm, marked as a default.

The PRD only says "coaching judgment". Following the PRD rules, the plan to call a model through an API, and the agent with its tools, is in the Forward block of shape-notes.md. /10x-tech-stack-selector reads it from there.
